The Garden of Forking Paths

To Victoria Ocampo

In his A History of the World War (page 212), Captain Liddell Hart reports that a planned offensive by thirteen British divisions, supported by fourteen hundred artillery pieces, against the German line at Serre-Montauban...

The following deposition, dictated by, read over, and then signed by Dr. Yu Tsun, former teacher of English at the Tsingtao Hochschule, casts unsuspected light upon this event.

. . . and I hung up the phone. Immediately I recollected the voice that had spoken in German. It was that of Captain Richard Madden.

I went up to my bedroom. Absurd though the gesture was, I closed and locked the door. I threw myself down on my narrow iron bed, and waited on my back.

Then I reflected that all things happen, happen to one, precisely now. Century follows century, and things happen only in the present.

In the midst of my hatred and terror, I knew that the fast-moving and doubtless happy soldier did not suspect that I possessed the Secret.

I sat up on the bed, in senseless and perfect silence, as if Madden was already peering at me.

The advice about turning always to the left reminded me that such was the common formula for finding the central courtyard of certain labyrinths.

"The Garden of Forking Paths is an enormous guessing game, or parable, in which the subject is time."

I fired with the utmost care: Albert fell without a murmur, at once. I swear that his death was instantaneous, as if he had been struck by lightning.
